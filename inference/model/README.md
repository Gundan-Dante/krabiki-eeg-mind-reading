| array | meaning |
|---|---|
| `whiteners` | per-timepoint Sigma_t^-1/2 (Ledoit-Wolf on the encoding model's out-of-fold training residuals) |
| `x_mean / x_std` | mean / std of the whitened, flattened EEG window over the subject's training trials |
| `f_mean / f_std` | mean / std of the feature over the subject's training videos (the candidates are standardized with them) |
| `B / y_mean` | ridge map and intercept at the chosen alpha (pooled members: from the weighted pooled ridge at the chosen lambda); prediction = X @ B + y_mean |
| `seed<k>_W_e / W_v / tau` | contrastive projections of the EEG window and of the video feature (cosine in the shared space); tau is stored for completeness and does not enter the ranking |
